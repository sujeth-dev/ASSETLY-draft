An asset earns by working, not by being owned.

Ownership hands you three things you never asked for — the upfront outlay, the depreciation curve, and the resale problem four years from now.

So we keep the residual, and you keep the cash.

Assetly funds the equipment and carries the obsolescence risk. You pay a fixed rental against the output it produces — nothing more.

Your credit lines stay exactly where they were.

No margin money, no fresh collateral, minimal movement in Debt/Equity. The facility you were saving for the next opportunity is still there.
